# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-22

- Introduced Campus Companion, the generalized standalone edition of Student Sticky.
- Added a dependency-free Node/SQLite planner, Today/Tomorrow blocks, priorities, manual reminders, local search and transparent grades.
- Preserved known-mark-only averages, bonus percentages, alternate scheme scenarios and explicit conflict handling.
- Included responsive Home Screen web app support and a generalized iPhone widget/reminder companion.
- Added private source imports, compact context, two AI workflow skills, setup/deployment/troubleshooting guides and fictional screenshots.
- Kept personal account data, grades, timetables, school documents and deployment secrets out of the distribution.
